Turn the server on in the list, then pick the groups whose members may use it. Administrators always can. Each user turns it on for their account, with a folder and a password of their own, in the Connect section above. Uploads arrive on the port of this web server, so no port has to be opened.
Taking a group away refuses the uploads of its members. Their settings and files stay.
