Commands

            
Press T to open the chat line, or / to start a command right away. Tab completes
                command and item names, Up recalls earlier commands. Use ~ for your own position,
                e.g. /tp ~ ~20 ~. Commands other than /help and /seed need cheats turned on.

How to Play

            

                
W A S D move, Space jump, Shift sneak, Ctrl or double tap W to sprint

                
Left mouse mine a block, Right mouse place a block, use a workbench / furnace / chest, or eat

                
1 - 9 or the mouse wheel pick a hotbar slot, Middle click a block to take it in hand

                
E inventory and 2 x 2 crafting, Q drop an item (Ctrl+Q the whole stack)

                
T chat, / type a command such as /give or /tp

                
Right click a bed at night to sleep until morning, doors and gates to open them

                
Creative: double tap Space to fly, Space / Shift to rise and sink

                
F3 debug info, F1 hide the interface, Esc pause

            

            
Start by punching a tree for logs, turn them into planks on the 2 x 2 grid in your inventory (E), and
                build a workbench from four planks: it gives you a 3 x 3 grid. The recipe book next to the grid shows
                every pattern and lays it out for you. A wooden pickaxe
                mines stone; stone tools mine iron, iron tools mine gold and crystal. Smelt ores in a furnace.
                Keep your food bar up: apples drop from leaves, seeds from tall grass grow into wheat for bread,
                and two mushrooms with a bowl make stew. Harder difficulties make you hungry faster.
