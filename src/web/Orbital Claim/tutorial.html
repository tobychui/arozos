How to Play

Orbital Claim · mine the field, wire the grid, hold the line

The Loop

Mine → Power → Defend.

You lose only when every structure is gone.

Power

Solar makes it. Relay carries it. Everything else spends it.

Thick, bright links carry more.

Mining

Miners work every rock in range.

Rocks run dry. Faster flashes = more ore.

Links

Relays and solars branch. Miners and weapons don't.

Build your network out of relays.

Building

Out of reach = no power = no progress.

Reach it with a relay and it starts itself. Lost one? Click Show on the “isolated” warning to jump to it.

Balance

Demand over supply slows everything.

Batteries absorb spikes first. Yellow links = that grid is short.

Weapons

Beam never misses. Pulser outranges. Missiles splash.

Missiles spend minerals per shot.

Threats

Each one punishes a different lazy answer.

Mix your defences.

Fighter weaves Swarmer many Missile outranges lasers, homing shots Exploder rams Ring shielded Mothership boss, fires missiles

Waves

Arrows show where they'll come from.

Late waves arrive from several sides at once.

Upgrades

More blades, more power, same footprint.

Batteries upgrade once, to 4× storage. Weapons and repair stations pick a path on their first upgrade — and stay on it.

Pulser Paths

Pick one: snipe, shred, or shield.

Aegis stops shooting ships. It shoots down enemy missiles instead and never misses, but drains heavy power recharging after each one. Its upgrades shorten the recharge.

Repair

Drones fly out, patch, and come home.

Hurt drones retreat and recharge on the pad.
